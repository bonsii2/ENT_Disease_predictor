About the Project

Understanding AI in Otolaryngological Medical Image Analysis

Project Purpose

MediScan AI is an educational decision-support platform designed to demonstrate how deep learning models classify medical images. Built specifically for programming students and healthcare AI researchers, it demonstrates how computer vision can aid clinical workflow analysis.

The platform evaluates images across three distinct otolaryngological domains: Ear, Nose, and Throat diseases.

Why Artificial Intelligence?

Otolaryngology relies heavily on visual inspection using otoscopes, endoscopes, and oral examination. Machine learning algorithms (such as Convolutional Neural Networks and EfficientNet architectures) excel at identifying fine visual patterns, color changes, and textural anomalies in high-resolution medical imagery.

AI assist tools can serve as preliminary triage aids, assisting medical trainees in understanding feature recognition and probability distributions.

How Image Classification Works

When an image is submitted, it undergoes a standardized computer vision pipeline:

2. Preprocessing (224x224 RGB)
➔

➔
4. Softmax Classification
➔
5. Prediction & Recommendation
  • Preprocessing: Images are normalized, resized to 224x224 pixels, and converted into 4D tensor arrays.
  • Inference: The trained model extracts features and outputs confidence logits.
  • Softmax Layer: Converts raw scores into percentage probabilities across all possible classes.

The Three Prediction Models

  • Ear Disease Predictor: Classifies otoscopic images into Acute Otitis Media, Cerumen Impaction, Chronic Otitis Media, Myringosclerosis, or Normal eardrum status.
  • Nose Disease Predictor: Configurable classifier identifying Allergic Rhinitis, Nasal Polyps, or Normal mucosa.
  • Throat Disease Predictor: Classifies oral cavity imagery into Pharyngitis, Tonsillitis, or Normal throat features.
